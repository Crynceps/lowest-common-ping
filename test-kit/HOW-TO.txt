LOWEST COMMON PING - TEST BUILD
===============================

Lowest Common Ping shows which world has the lowest ping for everyone in your RuneLite party.
It is not on the Plugin Hub yet, so this kit runs it with the RuneLite you already have.

Nothing gets installed. It does use your normal RuneLite settings, so things you change while
testing (like switching on the Party plugin) stay that way in your normal RuneLite.

BEFORE YOU START
  - This test build has not been reviewed by RuneLite yet; that happens when it goes on the
    Plugin Hub. Use it only with your own account on your own PC, at your own risk.
  - Nobody, including whoever sent you this, will ever need your password or any file from
    your .runelite folder (except client.log, if something goes wrong).
  - Your pings show the party roughly which region you are in. To only watch, turn off
    "Share pings with party" in the plugin's settings.

You need: a Windows PC with RuneLite installed that you have started at least once
(starting it from the Jagex Launcher counts). Close RuneLite before you start.


1. EXTRACT THE ZIP
------------------
  a. Right-click the zip file > "Extract All..." > "Extract".
     (With 7-Zip or WinRAR, use its "Extract to LowestCommonPing-test\" option.)
     Double-clicking the zip only shows what is inside; the kit does not work from there.
  b. In the folder that opens, open the "LowestCommonPing-test" folder.

  Windows may warn you the first time you open each .bat file in this kit, because they
  come from a download and are not signed:
    - "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
    - "Open File - Security Warning" ("The publisher could not be verified"): click "Run".
  To avoid these warnings: before extracting, right-click the zip > Properties > tick
  "Unblock" at the bottom > OK.


2. START IT
-----------
  a. Double-click "Start Lowest Common Ping".
  b. A black window opens and fills with technical text, then RuneLite opens. Lines with
     WARN or ERROR in that text are normal. Leave the black window open while you play,
     but don't click inside it: on some PCs that pauses RuneLite until you press Esc.
     Closing the black window closes RuneLite.


3. JOIN THE PARTY (you do not need to log in for this)
------------------------------------------------------
  a. The sidebar is the column of small icons on the right edge of RuneLite (press Ctrl+F11
     if you don't see it). Click the wrench at the top of it, type Party in the search box,
     and click the switch next to the plugin called just "Party" (the first result) so it
     turns on.
  b. Click the Party icon (three white figures) that appears in the sidebar, click
     "Join party", paste the passphrase you were sent (no spaces before or after it) and
     click OK.

  If you stay on the login screen for more than 30 minutes, RuneLite leaves the party by
  itself. Click the Party icon, then "Join previous party" to get back in.


4. OPEN LOWEST COMMON PING
-------------------------
  Click the Lowest Common Ping icon in the sidebar (green and orange signal bars) and keep
  that panel open. Measuring every world takes about 2 minutes (longer on some networks);
  "You measured ... of ... worlds" at the top shows how far it is.

  Everyone in the party who also runs Lowest Common Ping gets a column. "You" is you; the
  others show their in-game name once they log in ("Member" before that). The worlds that
  suit everyone best are at the top.


5. OPTIONAL: LOG IN TO THE GAME (to try hopping and the in-game overlay)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  - If you log in to RuneLite with a username/email and password: just log in.

  - If you have a Jagex account (you start the game from the Jagex Launcher), RuneLite needs
    a saved login token first. This uses RuneLite's official method for developers; it is
    optional and at your own risk, and Jagex does not officially support it. You can test
    everything except hopping and the overlay without it. To set it up:
      a. Close RuneLite, including the one started by this kit.
      b. Double-click "Jagex login - set up" and follow what it says
         (paste one line into a RuneLite settings window, click Save).
      c. Start RuneLite once through the Jagex Launcher as you normally do.
         When the RuneLite login screen appears, close it; you don't need to log in.
      d. Double-click "Start Lowest Common Ping" again. You can now log in without a password.
      e. A restart leaves the party: click the Party icon, then "Join previous party".
         The worlds are measured again, which takes another couple of minutes.

    IMPORTANT: that token can log in to your account without a password, so never share the
    file it creates (credentials.properties in your .runelite folder). Until you clean up
    (step 6), every start through the Jagex Launcher saves a fresh token. If you think someone
    else got that file, use "End sessions" in your account settings on runescape.com; that
    makes the token useless.


6. WHEN YOU ARE DONE
--------------------
  Close RuneLite. If you did the Jagex account steps, first double-click "Jagex login - clean up"
  and follow what it says: it removes the setting and deletes the token. After that you can
  delete the extracted folder.


SOMETHING WRONG?
----------------
  Send a screenshot of the black window to whoever sent you this kit.
  If RuneLite started but misbehaves, also send the file client.log from this folder
  (paste the line into the File Explorer address bar and press Enter):
      %USERPROFILE%\.runelite\logs
